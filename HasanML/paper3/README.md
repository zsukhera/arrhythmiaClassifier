# paper3 — Farag (2023) Tiny Matched-Filter 1D-CNN

## Citation

> M. M. Farag, "A Tiny Matched Filter-Based CNN for Inter-Patient ECG
> Classification and Arrhythmia Detection at the Edge," *Sensors*, vol. 23,
> no. 3, art. 1365, 2023, doi: [10.3390/s23031365](https://doi.org/10.3390/s23031365).

## What this replicates

A very compact 1D-CNN (~1.2–1.6k parameters, ~15 KB TFLite) that classifies
single MIT-BIH heartbeats into AAMI classes using the de Chazal DS1/DS2
inter-patient split.  The key innovation is **matched-filter initialisation**:
the Conv1D kernels are initialised from class-average waveform templates
computed on the training set, giving the network a strong prior aligned with
ECG morphology.

**Our extension** (not in the paper): a binary Normal vs. Arrhythmia classifier
using the same architecture, evaluated with both a dedicated binary model and by
collapsing the 3-class model's output.

## Accuracy definitions — important

The paper reports **"average accuracy"**, which is ambiguous.  In a related
paper we replicated (Yakut 2020), "average accuracy" turned out to mean the
**mean one-vs-rest (OvR) accuracy** = 1 − 2e/K, **not** plain multiclass
accuracy = 1 − e.  For K = 3 and OvR = 98.18 %, plain accuracy ≈ 97.27 %.

We compute both and report them side-by-side.  The per-class confusion matrix
in Table 4 is used to determine which definition matches.

We also always report the **majority-class baseline** (predict all N): N is
~89.5 % of DS2 beats (paper Sec 5.1), so a plain accuracy of e.g. 97 %
corresponds to only +7.5 pp over the trivial predictor.

## Architecture

```
ecg_in (NK, C)
  └── Conv1D(filters=13, kernel_size=NK, padding="valid", activation="relu")
  └── BatchNormalization
  └── GlobalMaxPooling1D ──────────────────────────────────► (13,)
                                                               │
rr_in (4,)                                                     │
  └── Dense(16, relu) → Dense(16, relu) → Dense(16, relu) ──► (16,)
                                                               │
                                              Concatenate ──► (29,)
                                                               │
                                       Dense(n_classes, softmax)
```

NK = 32 (default) or 64.  C = 1 (signal or derivative) or 2 (both).

Parameter counts (3-class, 1-channel):
| NK | Total | Trainable | Non-trainable |
|----|-------|-----------|---------------|
| 32 | 1,195 |    1,169  |            26 |
| 64 | 1,611 |    1,585  |            26 |

## Assumptions

All ASSUMPTIONs are configurable in `configs/default.yaml`.

| # | Assumption | Default | Notes |
|---|-----------|---------|-------|
| 1 | Resampling method | `resample_poly(up=16, down=45)` | Paper does not specify |
| 2 | Segment centre | 32 before + 32 after R-peak | Paper says 64 samples total |
| 3 | Edge-padding | `np.pad(mode='edge')` | Paper says "edge-padded" |
| 4 | Normalisation | Subtract segment mean | Paper mentions "mean" in MF computation |
| 5 | Derivative padding | Append last diff value | Paper does not specify |
| 6 | RR features | 4 (pre/post × local/global) | Paper Sec 3 describes these |
| 7 | 13 templates | N, L, R, e, j, A, a, J, S, V, E, F, Q | Inferred from paper Figure 4 |
| 8 | Template source | DS1-train only | Paper says "DS1"; we use train-only to avoid val leakage |
| 9 | Template norm | Unit L2 | Paper does not specify |
| 10 | Model padding | `"valid"` | Paper does not specify |
| 11 | Activation | ReLU | Paper says "ReLU or Tanh" |
| 12 | RR branch | 3 × Dense(16) | Paper Table 3 [assumption] |
| 13 | LR schedule | ReduceLROnPlateau, factor=0.5, patience=5 | Paper says "adaptive" |
| 14 | EarlyStopping | patience=20 | Paper says "rarely >100 epochs" |
| 15 | Class weight formula | sklearn `compute_class_weight("balanced")` | Paper mentions class weights |
| 16 | QAT framework | tensorflow_model_optimization (tfmot) | Paper used QKeras |
| 17 | Pruning | 50% magnitude sparsity | Paper mentions 50% pruning |

## Firmware checklist (ESP32 TFLite Micro)

The firmware must reproduce exactly:
1. **128 Hz resampling** of the raw 360 Hz ADC signal (or capture at 128 Hz directly)
2. **64-sample window** centred on detected R-peak (32 before, 32 after)
3. **Mean removal** per segment: subtract the mean of the 64 samples
4. **First derivative** (if using derivative input): `diff + edge-pad`
5. **NK=32 crop**: keep central 32 samples (indices 16–47)
6. **RR ring buffer** (400 entries) of R-peak timestamps at 128 Hz
7. **RR features** = [pre/local, pre/global, post/local, post/global] with
   80-beat and 400-beat causal moving averages
8. **Input quantisation**: use the scale and zero-point from the int8 TFLite model

The `.tflite` and `.h` files for the firmware are in `results/export/`.
